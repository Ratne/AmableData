# Changelog

Versions follow `version` in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Each version is a released bundle;
installations get it, and the manifest changes, through the catalog.

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-22)

- "Powered by Mapbox" under the suggestions, also in the checkout address field.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

- Address autocomplete on the contact editor through Mapbox Geocoding v6, with the province in
  Italy and Spain and OpenStreetMap as the fallback.
- Settings screen for the Mapbox access token (checked before saving) and permanent geocoding.
